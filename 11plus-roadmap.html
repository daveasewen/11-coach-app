An AI-enhanced coaching and testing tool for Dave's son, preparing for the Kent Test and Bexley Selection Test in September 2026. Warm and encouraging for the student. Analytical and data-driven for the parent.

Built for one child. Architected to generalise without a rebuild. Don't build the generalisation — don't foreclose it.

Quality bar — antonyms

Single word. Directly opposite in meaning — not associatively related. Same or compatible part of speech. Age-appropriate for 10–11 year olds. If no clean antonym exists for a word, remove the antonym field rather than add a weak one.

Product Principles

05
The rules
Challenge any feature that can't trace back to one of these.
  • 01Exam-outcome driven. Every feature decision traces back to what moves the score. Bexley vocab gap is the primary target until September.
  • 02SLC/MLP standard. Ship lean, maintain quality, avoid premature complexity. Backlog items are explicitly tracked and deferred — not ignored.
  • 03AI enhances, does not enable. Core practice works without AI. AI adds the coaching layer. Graceful degradation is not optional.
  • 04Architecture supports generalisation. Don't build it. Don't foreclose it. Config is data, not logic.
  • 05Content quality over quantity. A weak antonym is worse than no antonym — it teaches the wrong thing. Get the data right before scaling.
  • 06Challenge drift. If a feature doesn't trace to exam outcome or a named principle, question it directly before building.
