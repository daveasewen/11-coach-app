*Synthesised from: Mathematics Familiarisation 1 (2021), Mathematics Familiarisation 2 (2021), NVR & Mathematics Test Booklet (2024)*
*Codes: 6853 915, 6853 917, 6853 948 | Date: May 2026 | Purpose: Inform app question bank design*

---

## Summary

GL Assessment 11+ Mathematics is tested across **three paper variants** in the familiarisation suite. Two are dedicated Mathematics-only papers (50 questions, ~50 minutes each) and one is the combined NVR & Mathematics paper (25 maths questions, ~30 minutes). All are multiple-choice A–E, no calculator.

Together these 125 questions cover the full KS2 National Curriculum with substantial repetition in core topics (place value, fractions, data handling, measurement) and a long tail of geometry, algebra, and problem-solving topics that appear only occasionally. The dedicated papers are significantly longer and harder than the NVR & Maths combined section — they go deeper into geometry, coordinates, transformation, and algebraic reasoning.

**The biggest opportunity**: ~60% of questions across all three papers are pure text/number — no diagram needed. These are the highest-ROI topics to build first.

## Priority Build Order

### Tier 1 — Text-only, fully generatable (build first)

All of these can be presented as plain text MCQ with no images:

- Number in figures/words
- Place value (digit value)
- Digit reordering
- Number sequences
- Division / missing number
- Equivalent fractions
- Ordering fractions
- Fraction of a quantity (multi-step)
- Powers and square numbers
- Roman numerals
- Mental arithmetic strategy (near-100, near-1000)
- Decimal operations and ordering
- Decimal-fraction conversion
- Negative numbers (text: "temperature is −3°C, rises 18°C")
- Algebra / inverse operations (2–3 step)
- Percentage of a quantity
- Ratio and proportion
- Rate/proportion word problems
- Unit conversion (length, mass, capacity, text-only)
- Time (24-hour, duration, time zones, overnight span)
- Money (change, rate, savings, multi-step)
- Perimeter (text description of rectangle)
- Area (text: "rectangle 6cm wide and 3cm tall")
- Index laws (once index notation is rendered)
- Km-to-miles formula application

**Estimated bank**: 20–30 questions per topic across 4 difficulty bands = **~500 questions** achievable without any image assets.

### Tier 2 — HTML-renderable, no image files needed

These require a data table or simple diagram but no pre-made images:

- Timetable questions (HTML table)
- Pricing / membership table (HTML table)
- Conversion graph (Canvas/SVG line with two axes)
- Number line (SVG with arrow markers)
- Negative number thermometer (SVG)
- Venn diagram (two overlapping SVG circles with numbers)
- Fraction of a shape (SVG triangle subdivided into equal parts)
- Angles on a straight line (SVG angle diagram)

### Tier 3 — Requires pre-made image assets or chart renderer

These need actual image assets or a chart library:

- Bar charts (Chart.js or pre-made PNG)
- Line graphs (Chart.js or pre-made PNG)
- Pie charts
- Pictograms
- Coordinate grids with plotted shapes (translation, reflection, scaling)
- Shape classification with 5 options (2D and 3D shapes)
- Shape tessellation

### Tier 4 — Deferred (complex diagram + reasoning)

- Perpendicular/parallel lines in rotated overlapping shapes
- Proportional bar chart (no y-axis, total given)
- Triangle inequality with stick lengths
- Multi-criteria number puzzle (prime + digit sum + square of number)

## Key Strategic Insights

**1. The dedicated Maths papers are significantly harder than the NVR & Maths combined section.** The combined paper tops out at ratio/proportion (Band 4). The dedicated papers push into index laws, interior angles formulas, coordinate transformations, and "which statement must/must not be true" meta-reasoning. For Alex's September deadline, targeting combined-paper difficulty (Band 1–3) is sufficient initially.

**2. The three papers have substantial topic overlap.** Place value, fractions, percentages, unit conversion, and data reading appear across all three. This overlap confirms which topics are highest frequency and therefore highest value to build.

**3. Geometry is the one area where diagrams are almost always required.** Shape classification (Q20 in M1, Q49 in NVR+M) requires showing 5 shapes. Coordinates requires a grid. Transformation requires a grid with shapes. This is why Tier 3/4 is dominated by geometry.

**4. The "must/must not be true" question type is a distinct skill.** Maths 2 Q24 and Q42 present a chart or scenario and ask which of 5 statements is definitely/not true. This tests careful reasoning, not just calculation. Worth including as its own sub-type.

**5. The dedicated Maths papers are 50 minutes / 50 questions — faster pace than they look.** The real test expects ~1 minute per question including reading time. Timed practice is important, and the app should track per-question response time.

**6. The combined NVR & Maths paper (2024) is the most recent and likely most representative of current Kent/Bexley format.** The dedicated Maths papers (2021) show the full range of what could appear but the combined paper format is what students will actually sit for Kent Test.
