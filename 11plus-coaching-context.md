# 11+ Coaching & Testing Schedule — Context Document  ## Document Purpose This document serves as the single source of truth for coaching preparation towards the Kent Test and Bexley Selection Test. It is designed to be shared with the tutor and used as a session handoff between coaching sessions.  ---  ## 1. Exam Landscape  ### 1.1 Kent Test (PESE) - **Format:** Two papers, each 1 hour, plus a 40-minute writing task - **Paper 1:** English (25 min) + Maths (25 min), with 5 min practice each - **Paper 2:** Reasoning (~1 hour) — Verbal Reasoning + Non-Verbal Reasoning (including spatial), split into timed subsections - **Writing:** 40 minutes (10 min planning). Only used in borderline cases by headteacher panel - **Scoring:** Three standardised scores (English, Maths, Reasoning) combined into an aggregate. Scores range 69–141 per paper, max total 423 - **Pass threshold (2024/25):** Total ≥ 332, no single score below 106–108 - **Weighting:** Equal across all three papers — no subject is weighted more heavily - **Provider:** GL Assessment - **Date:** Early September (typically 11 Sept for Kent schools, 13–14 Sept for out-of-county) - **Competition:** ~16,000 sit for ~5,600 places; top ~25% deemed grammar-suitable  ### 1.2 Bexley Selection Test - **Format:** Two papers, each ~50 minutes, all multiple choice - **Structure:** Each paper contains a MIX of all subjects (unlike Kent's separated papers) - **Subjects:** Verbal Ability (comprehension + VR), Numerical Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning - **Scoring:** Age-standardised scores per subject, then combined with weighting - **Weighting:** **50% Verbal Ability, 25% Numerical, 25% Non-Verbal** — verbal is DOUBLE weighted - **Pass threshold:** No fixed pass mark. Average standardised score is 200 (range 170–230 for most). Top ~30% deemed selective. Top 180 scorers get highest priority - **Provider:** GL Assessment (via Quest Assessments) - **Date:** Early September 2026 (between 2–10 Sept) - **Competition:** ~6,000 sit for ~800 places across 4 grammar schools  ### 1.3 Critical Difference: Weighting | Subject Area | Kent Weight | Bexley Weight | |---|---|---| | Verbal/English | 33% (equal) | **50%** | | Maths/Numerical | 33% (equal) | 25% | | Reasoning/NVR | 33% (equal) | 25% |  **Implication:** Vocabulary weakness is punished harder in Bexley. The verbal/English gap is the single most important area to close for both exams, but *doubly* so for Bexley.  ---  ## 2. Gap Analysis  ### 3.1 The Target - **Stated target:** 85% minimum - **Kent equivalent:** ~332/423 aggregate (approximately 111 per paper average) - **Bexley equivalent:** Standardised score significantly above 200 (likely 220+)  ### 3.2 The Gap by Domain (current → target) | Domain | Current | Target (85%) | Gap | Bexley Impact | |---|---|---|---|---| | Vocabulary | 39% | 85% | **+46pp** | Critical (50% weight) | | Comprehension/Cloze | 59% | 85% | +26pp | Critical (50% weight) | | Maths | 60% | 85% | +25pp | Moderate (25% weight) | | NVR | 58% | 85% | +27pp | Moderate (25% weight) | | Verbal Reasoning | 64% | 85% | +21pp | Part of verbal (50%) |  ### 3.3 Priority Stack (weighted by impact) 1. **Vocabulary** — Largest gap, highest Bexley weighting, feeds into all verbal sections 2. **Timing/Test Technique** — Cross-cutting issue suppressing all scores 3. **Comprehension** — Feeds into Bexley's 50% verbal component 4. **NVR** — Consistent practice yields reliable gains 5. **Maths** — Already near-target when time-managed; needs speed work not new knowledge  ---  ## 4. Coaching Strategy  ### 4.1 Phased Approach (April → September)  #### Phase 1: Foundation Building (April–May) — Weeks 1–8 **Focus:** Vocabulary acquisition + timing awareness + reading volume  - **Vocabulary:** 20 new words per week using spaced repetition (Leitner system with flashcards). Focus on high-frequency 11+ words. Each word: definition, synonym, antonym, example sentence - **Reading:** Daily 15–20 min reading programme. Mix fiction (classic children's literature) and non-fiction. Target: build passive vocabulary and comprehension speed - **Timing:** Introduce timed segments in practice — start at 1.5x allowed time, reduce by 10% each fortnight - **Maths:** Consolidate Year 5/6 foundations. Focus on speed drills for arithmetic (2–3 min daily) - **NVR:** Pattern recognition fundamentals. 15 min every other day  **Weekly vocab target by end of Phase 1:** 160 words learned and retained  #### Phase 2: Skill Integration (June–July) — Weeks 9–16 **Focus:** Practice papers under increasing time pressure + vocabulary deepening  - **Vocabulary:** Continue 20 words/week + weekly review of all previous words. Begin synonym/antonym pair drilling - **Practice Papers:** One section-based paper per week (not full mock). Rotate: Verbal → Maths → NVR → Comprehension - **Timing:** Move to actual exam timing for individual sections. Record time-per-question metrics - **Comprehension:** Introduce structured approach: skim → questions → scan → answer. Practice with unfamiliar text types - **Cloze Procedure:** Weekly cloze exercises using varied text difficulty - **Shuffled Sentences:** Daily 5-min exercises for sentence structure recognition  **Weekly vocab target by end of Phase 2:** 320 words cumulative  #### Phase 3: Exam Simulation (August) — Weeks 17–20 **Focus:** Full mock exams under real conditions + targeted gap filling  - **Full Mocks:** One per week, under strict exam conditions (correct timing, separate answer sheets, no breaks within papers) - **Analysis:** Score each mock, compare to baseline, identify remaining weak areas - **Vocabulary:** Maintenance reviews only + focus on words from mock errors - **Speed Work:** Intensive timing drills on weakest-speed sections - **Confidence Building:** Mix in easier material to maintain motivation alongside stretch content  **Cumulative vocab target:** 400+ words learned and retained  #### Phase 4: Final Preparation (September, pre-test) — Weeks 21–22 **Focus:** Light revision + mental preparation + logistics  - **Reduce intensity** — no new material in final week - **Review** flashcard highlights, favourite practice papers - **Exam logistics:** Familiarise with test centre, equipment (HB pencils, eraser), answer sheet format - **Wellbeing:** Adequate sleep, light exercise, low-stress activities  ### 4.2 Weekly Schedule Template | Day | Activity | Duration | Focus | |---|---|---|---| | Monday | Vocab + Maths speed drill | 30 min | New words + arithmetic fluency | | Tuesday | Comprehension or Cloze practice | 25 min | Reading skills | | Wednesday | NVR practice | 20 min | Pattern recognition | | Thursday | Vocab review + Shuffled Sentences | 25 min | Retention + verbal reasoning | | Friday | Timed section practice | 30 min | Exam technique | | Saturday | Tutor session | 60 min | Guided practice + feedback | | Sunday | Free reading + vocab game | 20 min | Fun engagement |  **Total guided study time:** ~3.5 hours/week (excluding tutor session and reading)  ### 4.3 Vocabulary Acquisition Method Based on spaced repetition research (Ebbinghaus forgetting curve):  1. **Day 1:** Learn 5 new words — definition, synonym, antonym, use in sentence 2. **Day 2:** Review Day 1 words + learn 5 new words 3. **Day 4:** Review all 10 words 4. **Day 7:** Review all words from the week 5. **Day 14:** Review again — words mastered move to monthly review pile 6. **Day 30:** Final review — words retained move to "known" pile  **Leitner Box System:** Physical flashcards in 5 boxes. Correct answers advance a box (reviewed less frequently). Incorrect answers return to Box 1.  ### 4.4 Timing Strategy The mock results show timing as a cross-cutting issue. Strategy:  1. **Awareness:** Time each section during practice. Record questions-per-minute rate 2. **The 30-Second Rule:** If stuck on a question for >30 seconds, mark best guess and move on 3. **Backward Planning:** With 25 questions in 25 minutes, target = 1 min per question. Practice to this cadence 4. **Strategic Guessing:** No negative marking in GL Assessment — always fill in an answer 5. **Section Scanning:** Read all questions first (10 seconds), identify easy wins, complete those first  ---  ## 5.
