## 1. Exam Landscape

### 1.1 Kent Test (PESE)
- **Format:** Two papers, each 1 hour, plus a 40-minute writing task
- **Paper 1:** English (25 min) + Maths (25 min), with 5 min practice each
- **Paper 2:** Reasoning (~1 hour) — Verbal Reasoning + Non-Verbal Reasoning (including spatial), split into timed subsections
- **Writing:** 40 minutes (10 min planning). Only used in borderline cases by headteacher panel
- **Scoring:** Three standardised scores (English, Maths, Reasoning) combined into an aggregate. Scores range 69–141 per paper, max total 423
- **Pass threshold (2024/25):** Total ≥ 332, no single score below 106–108
- **Weighting:** Equal across all three papers — no subject is weighted more heavily
- **Provider:** GL Assessment
- **Date:** Early September (typically 11 Sept for Kent schools, 13–14 Sept for out-of-county)
- **Competition:** ~16,000 sit for ~5,600 places; top ~25% deemed grammar-suitable

### 1.2 Bexley Selection Test
- **Format:** Two papers, each ~50 minutes, all multiple choice
- **Structure:** Each paper contains a MIX of all subjects (unlike Kent's separated papers)
- **Subjects:** Verbal Ability (comprehension + VR), Numerical Reasoning, Non-Verbal Reasoning
- **Scoring:** Age-standardised scores per subject, then combined with weighting
- **Weighting:** **50% Verbal Ability, 25% Numerical, 25% Non-Verbal** — verbal is DOUBLE weighted
- **Pass threshold:** No fixed pass mark. Average standardised score is 200 (range 170–230 for most). Top ~30% deemed selective. Top 180 scorers get highest priority
- **Provider:** GL Assessment (via Quest Assessments)
- **Date:** Early September 2026 (between 2–10 Sept)
- **Competition:** ~6,000 sit for ~800 places across 4 grammar schools

### 1.3 Critical Difference: Weighting
| Subject Area | Kent Weight | Bexley Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Verbal/English | 33% (equal) | **50%** |
| Maths/Numerical | 33% (equal) | 25% |
| Reasoning/NVR | 33% (equal) | 25% |

**Implication:** Vocabulary weakness is punished harder in Bexley. The verbal/English gap is the single most important area to close for both exams, but *doubly* so for Bexley.

**Profiles:**
- Birthdate-based age-gating
- Timed mode toggle (default + per-session override)
- Progress reset

### 4.3 v1.12 Soft Progression — Design (approved, in development)
Mastery-triggered difficulty band unlocks replacing static age-gating:

- **Leitner types** master when ≥50% of questions in boxes 4–6
- **Generated types** master when ≥80% correct across last 3 sessions
- **Per-type independent unlock** — each question type unlocks its own next band independently
- **Age sets starting band only** — after first launch, performance drives all band changes
- **Blend rate** — on unlock, 20% of session slots become next-band questions; auto-tunes ±5% per 20-question window
- **Auto-tuning is bidirectional (required):** rate increases if next-band accuracy ≥80%, decreases if <60%, steps back to current band if <60% for 2 consecutive windows
- **Parent dashboard** shows blend rate per type with manual override capability

## 5. Coaching Strategy

### 5.1 Phased Approach (April → September)

#### Phase 1: Foundation Building (April–May) — Weeks 1–8 ✅ IN PROGRESS
**Focus:** Vocabulary acquisition + timing awareness + reading volume

- **Vocabulary:** 20 new words per week using spaced repetition (Leitner system — now digital). Focus on high-frequency 11+ words.
- **Reading:** Daily 15–20 min reading programme. Mix fiction and non-fiction.
- **Timing:** Addressed via app timed mode (v1.11). Countdown timer, speed stats, coaching on slow questions.
- **App:** Daily sessions targeting 20–30 questions, mix of vocab and VR types

**Weekly vocab target by end of Phase 1:** 160 words learned and retained

#### Phase 2: Skill Integration (June–July) — Weeks 9–16
**Focus:** Practice papers under increasing time pressure + vocabulary deepening

- **Vocabulary:** Continue 20 words/week + weekly review. Soft progression (v1.12) begins blending harder words automatically.
- **Practice Papers:** One section-based paper per week. Rotate: Verbal → Maths → NVR → Comprehension
- **Timing:** Move to actual exam timing for sections. App speed trend chart tracks progress.
- **Comprehension:** Structured approach: skim → questions → scan → answer.

**Weekly vocab target by end of Phase 2:** 320 words cumulative

#### Phase 3: Exam Simulation (August) — Weeks 17–20
**Focus:** Full mock exams under real conditions + targeted gap filling

- **Full Mocks:** One per week under strict exam conditions
- **Analysis:** Score each mock, compare to app progress data
- **App:** NVR review (August 2026 decision point), maintenance sessions

**Cumulative vocab target:** 400+ words learned and retained

#### Phase 4: Final Preparation (September, pre-test) — Weeks 21–22
**Focus:** Light revision + mental preparation + logistics

- Reduce intensity — no new material in final week
- Review app dashboard to confirm strong areas
- **Exam logistics:** HB pencils, eraser, answer sheet format, test centre visit

## 7. Key Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Burnout / motivation loss | High | Keep sessions ≤20 min in app. Mix question types. Rest days sacred |
| Vocabulary plateau | Medium | App soft progression auto-advances difficulty; AI coaching prevents stagnation |
| Timing doesn't improve | Medium | App timed mode with speed trend chart tracks improvement objectively |
| Mock scores fluctuate | High | Normal. Focus on trend, not individual scores |
| Test anxiety on day | Medium | Simulate exam conditions in app. Timed mode mirrors real pressure. Visit test centre |
| App progression too fast | Low | Parent dashboard blend-rate override; auto-tuning pulls back if struggling |
